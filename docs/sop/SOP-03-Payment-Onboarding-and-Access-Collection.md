# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 03 — Payment, Onboarding & Access Collection

## Primary Owner
Project Manager / Client Success Manager

## Purpose
Convert the new sale into a properly prepared client account.

## Main Process
1. Confirm payment.
2. Send welcome communication.
3. Send onboarding form.
4. Collect business information.
5. Collect brand assets.
6. Collect platform access.
7. Test access.
8. Identify missing permissions.
9. Schedule kickoff call.
10. Confirm client contacts.
11. Confirm communication method.
12. Document onboarding blockers.

## Completion
Client information, critical assets, access, payment, and kickoff requirements are complete.
