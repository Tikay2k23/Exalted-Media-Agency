# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 03 — Payment, Onboarding & Access Collection

## Primary Owner
Project Manager / Client Success Manager

## Purpose
Convert the new sale into a properly prepared client account.

## Main Process
1. Confirm payment.
2. Record the contract start date and monthly value on the account.
3. Send welcome communication.
4. Send the intake form.
5. Collect business information.
6. Collect brand assets.
7. Collect platform access.
8. Test access.
9. Identify missing permissions.
10. Schedule kickoff call.
11. Confirm client contacts, and which of them may approve work.
12. Confirm communication method.
13. Document onboarding blockers.

## The intake form

Step 4 sends what this document previously called the onboarding form. The
system calls it the intake form; they are the same thing. It is sent to a named
contact, and re-sending rotates its link so a form that reached the wrong
person stops working.

## A2P registration

Where the account will send SMS, the Automation Specialist registers it before
anything sends. Complete the messaging profile and the sample messages, then
submit.

Internal readiness is not carrier approval. An account is not cleared to send
because the form was finished — the registration has to come back approved, and
the system keeps the two states apart on purpose.

## Completion
Client information, critical assets, access, payment, and kickoff requirements are complete.
