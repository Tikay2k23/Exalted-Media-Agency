# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 08 — Ongoing Client Management, Reporting & Retention

## Primary Owner
Project Manager / Client Success Manager

## Supporting Owners
- Ads, Tracking & Reporting Specialist
- GoHighLevel & Automation Specialist
- Website, Funnel, Design & Copy Specialist
- Agency Owner / Strategist

## Purpose
Manage the client after launch and continuously improve results.

## Main Process
1. Monitor client performance.
2. Send weekly updates.
3. Prepare monthly reports.
4. Review KPIs.
5. Review lead quality.
6. Review sales performance.
7. Review CRM performance.
8. Review campaign performance.
9. Record optimizations.
10. Monitor client health.
11. Resolve complaints.
12. Create recovery plans.
13. Monitor payments.
14. Identify retention risks.
15. Prepare future recommendations.

## Client Health
- Green
- Yellow
- Red

## Completion
Client remains actively managed until renewal, expansion, completion, or cancellation.
