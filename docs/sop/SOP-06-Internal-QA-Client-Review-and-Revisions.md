# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 06 — Internal QA, Client Review & Revisions

## Primary Owner
Project Manager

## QA Owners
Shared between qualified team members.

## Purpose
Verify quality before client approval and launch.

## Main Process
1. Review approved scope.
2. Test deliverables.
3. Test customer journey.
4. Record defects.
5. Assign severity.
6. Assign correction owner.
7. Correct defects.
8. Retest.
9. Complete regression testing.
10. Send client review.
11. Collect consolidated feedback.
12. Classify feedback.
13. Complete included revisions.
14. Identify out-of-scope requests.
15. Obtain final client approval.

## Defect Severity
- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

## Feedback Types
- Defect
- Factual Correction
- Included Revision
- Preference
- Enhancement
- Scope Change
- Question
- Rejected

## QA Rule
The person who created a deliverable must not be the only person who gives final QA approval.

## Completion
QA passes and final client approval is documented.
