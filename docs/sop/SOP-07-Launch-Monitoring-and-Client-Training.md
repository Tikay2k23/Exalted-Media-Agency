# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 07 — Launch, Monitoring & Client Training

## Primary Owner
Project Manager

## Technical Owners
Relevant specialists.

## Purpose
Launch the approved system safely and train the client.

## Main Process
1. Confirm final approval.
2. Confirm backup.
3. Confirm rollback plan.
4. Confirm launch owner.
5. Activate required systems.
6. Publish pages.
7. Activate workflows.
8. Activate integrations.
9. Verify tracking.
10. Activate campaigns.
11. Run live end-to-end test.
12. Monitor first 2 hours.
13. Monitor first 24 hours.
14. Monitor first 72 hours.
15. Complete 7-day review.
16. Resolve incidents.
17. Train client.
18. Deliver training resources.

## Completion
System is Live & Stable and client training is complete.
