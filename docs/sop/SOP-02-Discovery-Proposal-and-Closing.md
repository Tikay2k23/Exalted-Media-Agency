# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 02 — Discovery, Proposal & Closing

## Primary Owner
Sales Representative

## Supporting Owner
Agency Owner / Strategist

## Purpose
Understand the prospect, recommend the correct service, send the proposal, and close the deal.

## Main Process
1. Book discovery call.
2. Complete discovery.
3. Document business problem.
4. Document goals.
5. Confirm budget.
6. Confirm timeline.
7. Confirm decision-maker.
8. Recommend service.
9. Prepare proposal.
10. Send proposal.
11. Follow up.
12. Handle objections.
13. Send agreement.
14. Send payment request.
15. Mark Won or Lost.
16. Complete sales handoff.

## Completion
Agreement and required payment are received, then the client moves to onboarding.
