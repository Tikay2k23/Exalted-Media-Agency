# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 09 — Renewal, Upsell, Testimonials, Referrals & Offboarding

## Primary Owner
Project Manager / Client Success Manager

## Strategic Owner
Agency Owner / Strategist

## Sales Support
Sales Representative

## Purpose
Manage the final commercial stage of the client relationship.

## Renewal Process
1. Review performance.
2. Review client health.
3. Review profitability.
4. Review future goals.
5. Prepare renewal recommendation.
6. Hold renewal discussion.
7. Send proposal.
8. Confirm decision.
9. Update agreement and billing.

## Expansion
Identify:
- Upsells
- Cross-sells
- Additional locations
- Additional services

Only recommend services that solve a real business need.

## Testimonials
Request when:
- Client is satisfied.
- Strong result exists.
- Project completed successfully.
- Client renewed.

Always record publishing permission.

## Referrals
Request from satisfied clients and record the referral source.

## Offboarding
1. Confirm final service date.
2. Confirm final billing.
3. Complete remaining work.
4. Transfer approved assets.
5. Export required data.
6. Transfer client ownership.
7. Remove agency access.
8. Pause or transfer workflows.
9. Pause or transfer campaigns.
10. Review subscriptions.
11. Deliver final report.
12. Confirm support end date.
13. Archive records.

## Completion
Client renews, expands, or is professionally offboarded.
