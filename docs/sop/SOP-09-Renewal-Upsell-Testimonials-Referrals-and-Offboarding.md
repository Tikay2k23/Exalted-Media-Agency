# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 09 — Renewal, Upsell, Testimonials, Referrals & Offboarding

## Primary Owner
Project Manager / Client Success Manager

## Strategic Owner
Agency Owner / Strategist

## Sales Support
Sales Representative

## Purpose
Manage the final commercial stage of the client relationship.

## Renewal Process
1. Record the renewal date on the account.
2. Review performance.
3. Review client health.
4. Review profitability.
5. Review future goals.
6. Prepare renewal recommendation.
7. Hold renewal discussion.
8. Send proposal.
9. Confirm decision.
10. Update agreement and billing.

## Expansion
Identify:
- Upsells
- Cross-sells
- Additional locations
- Additional services

Only recommend services that solve a real business need.

## Testimonials
Request when:
- Client is satisfied.
- Strong result exists.
- Project completed successfully.
- Client renewed.

Always record publishing permission.

## Referrals
Request from satisfied clients and record the referral source.

## Offboarding
1. Confirm final service date.
2. Settle final billing.
3. Finish or write off remaining work.
4. Hand over client-owned assets.
5. Export client data.
6. Confirm the client is an administrator on their own platforms.
7. Remove agency access.
8. Pause or transfer workflows and campaigns.
9. Review subscriptions.
10. Send the final report.
11. Confirm the support end date.
12. Archive the account.

## The order of steps 6 and 7

Confirm the client holds administrator access to their own platforms **before**
agency access is removed. Not after, and not at the same time.

If agency access goes first and nobody at the client is an administrator, they
are locked out of their own accounts permanently, and getting back in means a
support case with each platform. This is the one step in offboarding with a
consequence that cannot be undone from inside the system, which is why the
software will not let step 7 be recorded before step 6.

## Archive, not delete

Step 12 is Archive. It takes the account off the active lists and the dashboard
counts and keeps every record it ever had, and it can be reversed.

The client record also carries a Delete action, which destroys the account and
everything attached to it and cannot be undone. It is not part of offboarding.
Use it only for an account that should never have existed — a duplicate, or a
test row.

## Completion
Client renews, expands, or is professionally offboarded.
