# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 01 — Lead Capture & Qualification

## Primary Owner
Sales Representative

## Purpose
Ensure every new lead is captured, assigned, contacted, qualified, and given a
clear next action so no legitimate opportunity is lost or left unattended.

## Trigger
This SOP begins when a new prospect enters the agency through an approved
source such as the website, advertising, referral, manual entry, or a connected
lead source.

## Scope
Applies from initial lead capture until the prospect is classified as Qualified,
Long-Term Nurture, or Lost / Unqualified and is either handed to SOP-02 or
closed appropriately.

## Outcomes
- Lead is recorded in the Sales system
- Duplicate status has been checked
- Lead source is known
- Sales owner is assigned
- Valid contact information is available
- Business need and service interest are understood
- Budget, timeline, and decision-maker information are captured where possible
- Every open lead has a Next Action
- Every open lead has a Next Follow-Up
- Lead is routed to the correct final outcome
- Qualified opportunities are ready for SOP-02

## Entry Criteria
- A new inquiry or prospect exists
- At least one usable contact method is available
- The lead has an identifiable source
- The record is not confirmed as an existing duplicate
- The lead falls within an approved sales / marketing intake channel

## Agency Standard
Every actionable lead must have a clear owner, current stage, Next Action, and
Next Follow-Up. Leads must not remain unattended or indefinitely open without a
documented reason.

### Ownership
Every active lead has one accountable Sales Representative.

### Follow-Up
Every active lead has a dated Next Follow-Up.

### Qualification
A lead should not progress to Discovery until sufficient qualification
information exists.

### Data Integrity
Duplicate leads must be resolved rather than worked as separate opportunities.

## Typical Outcome
Qualified opportunity, or a properly closed or nurtured lead.

## Main Process
1. Capture lead.
2. Record lead source.
3. Assign salesperson.
4. Verify contact information.
5. Record service interest.
6. Check budget.
7. Check urgency.
8. Confirm decision-maker.
9. Score lead.
10. Schedule follow-up.
11. Qualify or disqualify lead.

## Lead Status
- New
- Attempting Contact
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Disqualified
- Nurture

## Completion
Lead is qualified and moves to Discovery, or is properly marked Disqualified or Nurture.
