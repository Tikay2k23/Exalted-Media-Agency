# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 01 — Lead Capture & Qualification

## Primary Owner
Sales Representative

## Purpose
Ensure every new lead is captured, assigned, contacted, qualified, and given a
clear next action so no legitimate opportunity is lost or left unattended.

## Trigger
This SOP begins when a new prospect enters the agency through an approved
source such as the website, advertising, referral, manual entry, or a connected
lead source.

## Scope
Applies from initial lead capture until the prospect is classified as Qualified,
Long-Term Nurture, or Lost / Unqualified and is either handed to SOP-02 or
closed appropriately.

## Outcomes
- Lead is recorded in the Sales system
- Duplicate status has been checked
- Lead source is known
- Sales owner is assigned
- Valid contact information is available
- Business need and service interest are understood
- Budget, timeline, and decision-maker information are captured where possible
- Every open lead has a Next Action
- Every open lead has a Next Follow-Up
- Lead is routed to the correct final outcome
- Qualified opportunities are ready for SOP-02

## Entry Criteria
- A new inquiry or prospect exists
- At least one usable contact method is available
- The lead has an identifiable source
- The record is not confirmed as an existing duplicate
- The lead falls within an approved sales / marketing intake channel

## Agency Standard
Every actionable lead must have a clear owner, current stage, Next Action, and
Next Follow-Up. Leads must not remain unattended or indefinitely open without a
documented reason.

### Ownership
Every active lead has one accountable Sales Representative.

### Follow-Up
Every active lead has a dated Next Follow-Up.

### Qualification
A lead should not progress to Discovery until sufficient qualification
information exists.

### Data Integrity
Duplicate leads must be resolved rather than worked as separate opportunities.

## Typical Outcome
Qualified opportunity, or a properly closed or nurtured lead.

## Main Process

### 1. Capture the Lead
Short: Capture
Owner: Sales Representative / Automation

Create or receive the new lead from an approved source: the website, Meta Ads,
Google Ads, a referral, manual entry, or a connected CRM or approved lead
source.

Inputs:
- Name
- Email or phone
- Company, if applicable
- Lead source
- Date received

Result: A valid lead record exists in Sales.

### 2. Check for Duplicates
Short: Duplicates
Owner: Sales Representative

Check whether the same person or company is already on file, comparing email,
phone, company, and existing opportunities. Where a duplicate exists, use the
authoritative existing record rather than working two separate opportunities.

Inputs:
- Email
- Phone
- Company
- Existing opportunities

Result: One authoritative prospect record exists.
Rule: Duplicate leads must be resolved rather than worked independently.

### 3. Validate Lead Information
Short: Validate
Owner: Sales Representative

Confirm the basic lead data is complete and usable, and correct obvious
formatting or data errors where it is appropriate to do so.

Inputs:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Company
- Lead source

Result: There is enough reliable information to attempt contact.

### 4. Assign Sales Owner
Short: Assign
Owner: Sales Manager / Agency Owner / Automation

Assign every actionable lead to one accountable Sales Representative.

Result: The lead has a clear Sales owner.
Rule: No active lead may remain unassigned.

### 5. Perform Initial Outreach
Short: Outreach
Owner: Assigned Sales Representative

Attempt contact within the agency's approved response-time standard, using
approved communication channels, and record the outcome of the attempt:
reached, no answer, voicemail, email sent, SMS sent, or invalid contact
information.

Result: The first outreach attempt is documented and the next action is clear.

### 6. Set Next Action and Follow-Up
Short: Follow-Up
Owner: Sales Representative

Give every open lead a Next Action and a Next Follow-Up date - call tomorrow,
send the requested information, book a discovery call, follow up after the
budget review.

Result: No open opportunity is left without a defined next step.
Rule: An active lead cannot sit in the pipeline without a documented reason and
a next follow-up.

### 7. Gather Qualification Information
Short: Qualify
Owner: Sales Representative

Establish enough to judge whether this is a real opportunity for the agency.

Inputs:
- Business problem
- Desired result
- Service interest
- Budget
- Timeline
- Decision maker
- Urgency
- Current solution or provider, where relevant

Result: Enough information exists to decide whether the opportunity is a fit.
Rule: A lead is not Qualified because the person replied. Qualification rests on
evidence.

### 8. Classify the Lead
Short: Classify
Owner: Sales Representative

Choose the outcome the evidence supports. Qualified: a reasonable need, fit,
authority, timeline and commercial potential. Needs More Information: possibly
suitable, but the qualification decision cannot yet be made. Long-Term Nurture:
a future fit whose timing or readiness is not immediate. Lost / Unqualified: not
a suitable fit, or no viable opportunity.

Result: The lead is routed on qualification evidence rather than assumption.

### 9. Book the Discovery Call
Short: Book Call
Owner: Sales Representative
Applies when: The lead is Qualified.

Schedule the next sales conversation and confirm the date, the time, who is
attending, and its purpose.

Result: The qualified prospect progresses toward SOP-02.

### 10. Complete the Handoff
Short: Handoff
Owner: Sales Representative

Verify the opportunity is genuinely ready, then transition it into SOP-02 —
Discovery, Proposal & Closing.

Inputs:
- Sales owner assigned
- Qualification complete
- Business problem documented
- Goal documented
- Service interest documented
- Next meeting or action confirmed

Result: SOP-01 is complete and the qualified prospect is ready for the next
sales process.

## Exceptions and Escalation

### Lead cannot be contacted
Continue the approved follow-up cadence before moving the lead to Nurture or
Lost according to policy.

### Duplicate record found
Use the authoritative existing contact or opportunity rather than working
duplicate records separately.

### High-value or unusual opportunity
Escalate to the Agency Owner where approval or special handling is required.

### Service outside agency scope
Do not promise delivery. Escalate the opportunity for review.

### Missing contact information
Do not classify the lead as Qualified until enough usable information exists.

## Lead Status
- New
- Attempting Contact
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Disqualified
- Nurture

## Completion
Lead is qualified and moves to Discovery, or is properly marked Disqualified or Nurture.
