# THE EXALTED MEDIA
# SOP 05 — Production & Implementation

## Primary Owner
Project Manager

## Execution Owners
- GoHighLevel & Automation Specialist
- Website, Funnel, Design & Copy Specialist
- Ads, Tracking & Reporting Specialist

## Purpose
Build all approved client deliverables.

## Main Process
1. Assign tasks.
2. Confirm deadlines.
3. Confirm dependencies.
4. Build deliverables.
5. Track work status.
6. Report blockers.
7. Maintain backups.
8. Document major changes.
9. Track scope.
10. Attach completion evidence.
11. Complete builder self-review.
12. Submit completed work for QA.

## Work Status

Work is open until somebody has reviewed it. These are the statuses in the
order work usually passes through them.

Open:
- Not Started
- Ready
- In Progress
- Waiting for Client
- Blocked
- Internal Review
- Revision Required

Closed:
- Approved
- Complete
- Cancelled

*Waiting for Internal Input* was removed on 2026-09-01. Blocked already records
what the work is waiting on, and two ways of saying stuck meant people chose
inconsistently.

## Ready for QA

Ready for QA is not a status on a single work item. It is the account moving to
the Internal Quality Assurance stage, and it becomes available when no
production work item is still open — every one is Approved, Complete or
Cancelled.

The distinction matters: QA in SOP 06 tests deliverables and the customer
journey, which are properties of the whole account rather than of one task. A
per-item "ready for QA" would let an account reach QA with half its work
unreviewed.

## Daily reporting

Every person files an end-of-day entry against the work they touched: what they
did, what is next, and anything in the way. The week that entry builds up is
reviewed in Weekly Work, and the weekly report is due Friday at 17:00.

## Workstreams

An account carries one workstream per service it bought, each with a named
specialist. Creating the client creates the workstreams its service calls for,
and retiring a service retires the stream rather than deleting it, so the work
done under it stays readable.

## Completion
All approved production work is complete and ready for independent QA.
